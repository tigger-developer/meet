## Original issue body

## Problem

Recurring schedules (#17/#18) are hard to validate without seeing when they actually fall, and `meet list` grows noisy as one-off rooms come and go.

## Requirements

1. `meet create` for a recurring room prints the next up to 6 occurrences (start/end, in the zone and UTC); if more exist, a following line notes there are more.
2. `meet list` with no `--room` defaults to rooms that are active now or in the future (one-off active/upcoming, and recurring rooms not past their `--ends`), excluding past and cancelled; explicit `--filter` still available.
3. `meet list --room <name>` lists that room's upcoming occurrences, up to 6 by default and up to N via a count/pager flag.
